Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your photo and signature

They never leave your device. Resizing happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — when you choose a file, it's read locally, processed locally, and offered back to you as a download. We don't receive it, store it, or have any way to see it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand traffic at a high level — roughly how many people visit and which pages get used. It collects standard technical details like approximate location (city-level, from IP address), browser, and device type. It never sees the images you resize, because those never reach a server. You can opt out using a browser extension such as Google's Analytics opt-out add-on, or your browser's tracking-protection settings.

Advertising

This site participates in Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show relevant ads and may collect data as described in Google's own advertising privacy policy. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies

Aside from the cookies Google Analytics and Google AdSense set, this site doesn't use its own. There's no login and no user profile to track.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the change reflected directly on this page.
